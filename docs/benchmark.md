# Methods and benchmark protocol

## Primary study | Overall SVGs

**Estimand.** Within a section, test whether the gene's expression distribution depends on location after accounting for specified technical exposures such as library size. Composition-driven biology counts as signal here. Restrict the primary score to a frozen section-specific, detectability-filtered gene universe. Save IDs, detection fractions and all method-specific failures. Use each caller's recommended count or normalized inputs, with a harmonized preprocessing sensitivity analysis. Compare rankings on a common eligible universe and report coverage on the full eligible universe. Apply the same inclusion and screening rules in null runs.

**Baselines.** Official SPARK-X, Squidpy Moran's I, nnSVG and Spanve. Add Geary's C, SpatialDE2 and rank-only SpaGFT as resources permit. Include HVG and random ranking as controls. Compare with mean percentile rank, reciprocal rank, intersection, union, majority, equal-weight Cauchy + BY, and Castl's three modules. Use the same supported callers for a harmonized comparison and report the official Castl configuration separately if its member set differs. Use raw p-values for the harmonized Cauchy comparator; reproduce official defaults for the published comparator. Evaluate the repository's lightweight kernel alongside these as a prototype.

### A. Family-weighted evidence with joint null calibration

1. Freeze caller families on development data, starting with graph autocorrelation, kernel dependence, GP variance models, and local distribution tests. Convert directed scores to within-method percentiles with midranks. Assign equal total weight to each active family and equal weight to its members. Require a common successful panel per calibration experiment. In sensitivity analysis, report a predeclared minimum family coverage and recalibrate every alternate panel separately.
2. Use the weighted mean percentile to reward agreement. Add a complementary score using the maximum of family-mean percentiles to protect strong single-family signals. Start with each separately, then predefine a mixture weight using development simulations. Include uniform per-caller weights and a duplicate-caller stress test. Any learned weights are fitted within development folds only.
3. Estimate a null distribution for the **whole** score by rerunning the same pipeline on joint null replicates. Start with model-based counts conditional on spot depths, batch and tissue mask, retaining realistic per-gene mean, dispersion and detection. Compare with a shared within-depth/technical-stratum permutation where exchangeability is defensible. Check spatially patterned depth, irregular masks and spatially structured technical covariates. A rigid coordinate rotation preserves pairwise distances and is unsuitable for nulling distance-based evidence.
4. Compute empirical upper-tail p-values with (1 + exceedances)/(B + 1) for gene-wise exchangeable null draws. Model-estimated or stratified pooled nulls require independent validation for calibration, especially at low detection. Use separate null draws for development and evaluation. Apply BY as the conservative reference when marginal nulls are valid, with BH as an empirical comparison. At genome scale, check attainable p-value resolution against the multiplicity threshold before interpreting empty lists. Report rank performance and diagnostic p-values when simulation resolution or null adequacy prevents inferential claims. Never interpret a Cauchy tail approximation as automatic finite-sample control.

### B. Gene-level uncertainty and an independent stability check

Within each slice, recompute all scores on shared perturbations across callers. Report rank median and 10th–90th percentile, selection frequency at a fixed top-k and at an independently calibrated discovery threshold, leave-one-family-out rank change and support per family. Use separate axes for depth thinning, random spot loss and contiguous block deletion. Record the perturbation distribution and successful runs per gene. Start with 20 replicates for the pilot and increase to 100 for retained settings, reporting Monte Carlo error across independent perturbation draws conditional on the slice. These frequencies describe selection under the specified perturbations. Use truth-known simulations to check whether stability predicts correctness, with separate reliability curves by signal pattern and detectability. Use held-out sections for replication rates. Compare against Castl's cross-caller selection frequency to isolate the effect of perturbing the data.

### C. Domain-marker extension

Preserve the branch's weighted co-association, pruning, count split, depth-aware marker tests, spatial gate and perturbation loop. Compare published strict SpaGCN-style rules, majority vote, single split with gate and the existing combined procedure. Compare hard core-spot filtering with soft membership effects as a follow-up. Infer regions from discovery counts, test on the other half, and report label alignment to anchors, domain purity and boundary sensitivity. Check sensitivity to dispersion misspecification and depth loss. Count splitting can approximate independent discovery/test counts under its model and cannot establish biological replication or remove nonspatial states by itself. A spatial gate and dependent per-domain tests need end-to-end validation. For gene–domain claims, define the multiplicity family over eligible pairs. Use marker direction and annotated-region replication in real data.

## Evaluation matrix

| Arm | Data and controls | Metrics and defensible interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Exact truth | Existing layered/blob NB simulator, independent scDesign3 fits, SRTsim sensitivity. Include gradients, focal bumps, sharp layers, multiscale and nonspatial states. Cross signal strength with two depths and depth gradients. | Macro-average precision (AP), precision/recall at matched k, recall at matched observed FDR, and rank–effect association within pattern. FDR is mean per-replicate FDP. Report false calls as well as FDP, using FDP = 0 for an empty list. |
| All-null and mixed-null | Fit noise/depth nuisance on development data, then generate independently seeded pure-null and signal-plus-null runs. | Null p-value ECDF by expression bin, rejection rates at several cutoffs, probability of any rejection in all-null runs and realized FDR on mixed runs. Report uncertainty around empirical rates. |
| Semi-synthetic | Plant gradients, focal patches and layer signals in synthetic background genes over real coordinates and exposure. Retain unmodified measured genes for realistic context. | Spike recovery with matched spike prevalence and effect sizes. Estimate false positives only among explicitly generated nulls. Treat other genes as unlabeled. |
| Human Visium | DLPFC, all 12 slices. Fixed marker panel and manual layer labels held out of tuning. Adjacent-pair and held-out-donor comparisons. | Rank concordance, top-k Jaccard on shared genes, direction/marker recovery and replication of calls at matched size. ARI or boundary accuracy only as downstream domain metrics. |
| Platform transfer | Mouse MERFISH and Slide-seqV2 where inputs and compute permit. Evaluate separately by platform, panel and tissue. | Robustness to thinning/spot loss, effect or rank replication where independent sections exist, failure rate, time and peak RAM. Targeted panels change prevalence and eligible genes. |
| Circularity stress test | Match the Kang reference construction where reproducible, and rerun agreement leaving the evaluated caller out. | Compare method-derived agreement against true AP on simulations to quantify reference distortion. Real-data overlap is explicitly concordance. |

Use identical perturbations and paired comparisons, with k = 100 and 500 for transcriptome-wide panels and top 5%/10% for restricted panels. Use 0.05 and 0.10 as nominal FDR targets. Fit and freeze specifications on development seeds and donors before evaluation. Hold out entire donor groups and keep adjacent sections together. The existing simulations are development evidence for the new ensemble. For inference use independent simulated tissues and resample donors or datasets as clusters, then sections within donors where useful. With only three DLPFC donors show individual donor results and avoid precise population claims. Report effect differences and intervals, plus per-pattern and per-platform failures. Technical replicates and genes are not independent benchmark samples.

**Decision rule.** On independent simulations, the proposed score should improve AP or recall over both SPARK-X and the best development-selected Castl mode at comparable realized FDR. Report paired intervals for both differences, and whether the empirical FDR and its interval are compatible with the target. Excess false discoveries, gains limited to one simulator, loss of focal sensitivity, or absence of improvement over Castl argue against the proposed additions. Confirm transfer with held-out-donor replication at matched list size. Ablate family weights, max-family component, matched nulls and perturbation summaries individually.

**Run order.** Reproduce the existing tests and inspect its simulation outputs. Freeze data releases and method versions, smoke test an annotated slice and a labeled simulation, then run mixed-null and all-null comparisons before scaling to held-out donors and platforms. Log seeds, commands, threads, software and data versions, wall time, RAM, score direction and call coverage. Keep missing metrics distinct from zero calls.
